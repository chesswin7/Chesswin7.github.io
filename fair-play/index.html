Fair Play Policy

Every move in every online game is validated by the ChessWin7 game server, and clocks are kept by the server, so results cannot be edited from a browser.

Not allowed

  • Chess engines, bots or any outside help during a game
  • More than one account per person, or playing against yourself
  • Agreeing results with another player (collusion)
  • Exploiting bugs or interfering with the site

Accounts that break these rules may be suspended. To report a player or a problem, email Support.chesswin7@gmail.com.
