Chess Time Controls Explained

In online chess each player has a clock. Your clock runs only during your turn; if it reaches zero you lose on time — unless your opponent no longer has enough pieces to checkmate, in which case the game is a draw.

Time controls on ChessWin7

  • UltraBullet — 30 seconds per player. Pure speed.
  • Bullet — 1 to 2 minutes per player. Fast instincts matter most.
  • Blitz — 3 to 5 minutes per player. The most popular online format.
  • Rapid — 10 to 60 minutes per player. Time to think and plan.
  • Classical — 60 minutes or more. Deep, careful chess.

Which one should I choose?

If you are learning, start with Rapid so you have time to think. Blitz is great once you know the openings. Bullet and UltraBullet reward quick pattern recognition.

Our game server keeps the official clock for both players, so the time shown is the real time left.
