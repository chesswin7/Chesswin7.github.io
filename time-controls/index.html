Chess Time Controls Explained

In online chess each player has a clock. Your clock runs only during your turn; if it reaches zero you lose on time — unless your opponent no longer has enough pieces to checkmate, in which case the game is a draw.

How the clock works on ChessWin7

  • White's clock starts as soon as the game begins.
  • When you make a move, your clock stops and your opponent's clock starts.
  • Our game server keeps the official clock for both players, so the time shown is the real time left — refreshing the page or a slow phone cannot change it.
  • If your time runs out you lose, unless your opponent cannot possibly checkmate (for example a lone king) — then it is a draw.

Tips for fast games

  • Play your openings quickly and save your time for the middlegame.
  • In Bullet, simple safe moves are often better than long calculations.
  • If you are short of time, keep your king safe and avoid complications.
