Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what ChessWin7 (operated by Zed-Alpha) collects and why.

What we collect

Your email address, username, optional phone number, game results and rating, and for payments the transaction IDs and wallet addresses you submit.

Why

To run your account and games, process deposits and withdrawals, prevent fraud and answer support requests.

Where it is stored

Accounts and data are stored with Google Firebase. Games run on our game server. Live chat is provided by Crisp. Videos on the homepage are played by YouTube.

What we never do

We do not sell your personal data. Other players can see only your username, rating and results.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data by emailing Support.chesswin7@gmail.com. Some payment records may need to be kept for fraud prevention and legal reasons.
