How to Play Chess Online on ChessWin7

ChessWin7 lets you play chess online in your browser, on phone or computer, with no download. Here is how to start.

1. Create your free account

Tap Sign Up, choose a username and enter your email and a password — or use Continue with Google. Open the verification link we email you, then return to the site.

2. Choose how you want to play

  • vs AI — practise against the computer, any time, with no opponent needed.
  • vs Friend — create a room and share the 6-character room code with a friend, or join a friend's room with their code.
  • Matchmaking — pick a time control and get paired with another player automatically.

3. Pick a time control

From 30-second UltraBullet to long Classical games. See chess time controls explained.

4. Make your moves

Tap a piece to see where it can go: grey dots show possible moves and a ring shows a piece you can capture. Tap the square to move. Every move is checked by our game server, so illegal moves are impossible.

5. Win, draw or resign

A game ends by checkmate, by running out of time, by resignation, by agreement to a draw, by stalemate, by threefold repetition, by the 50-move rule or by insufficient material. Your rating goes up when you win and down when you lose.
