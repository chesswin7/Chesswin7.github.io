How to Play Chess Online on ChessWin7

ChessWin7 lets you play chess online in your browser, on phone or computer, with no download. Here is how to start — it only takes a few taps.

1. Create your free account

Tap Sign Up, choose a username and enter your email and a password — or use Continue with Google. Open the verification link we email you, then return to the site. Your username is what other players see.

2. Choose how you want to play

♟

vs AI

Practise against the computer any time, with no opponent needed. Choose White, Black or Random.

♞

vs Friend

Choose the time and the entry, create a room and share the 6-character room code with a friend — or join a friend's room with their code.

♚

Matchmaking

Pick a time control and an entry, and get paired with another player who chose the same.

Entries and prizes

Games against other players have an entry fee that both players pay. The winner receives both entries minus a 5% platform fee (a 5-COIN entry pays the winner 9.50 COINS). A draw, or a game that ends before both players have moved, returns both entries. Games against the AI are free.

3. Pick a time control

From 30-second UltraBullet to 60-minute Classical games. See chess time controls explained.

4. Make your moves

Tap a piece to see where it can go: grey dots show the squares it can move to and a ring shows a piece you can capture. Tap the square to move.

The last move is highlighted on the board, so you always see what your opponent just played. When a pawn reaches the last row you choose its new piece: queen, rook, bishop or knight.

Every move is checked by our game server, so illegal moves are impossible.

5. Win, draw or resign

A game ends by checkmate, by running out of time, by resignation, by agreement to a draw, by stalemate, by threefold repetition, by the 50-move rule or by insufficient material. Your rating goes up when you win and down when you lose.

Tips for your first games

  • Control the centre. Pawns and pieces on the central squares (d4, e4, d5, e5) give you more space.
  • Develop your pieces. Bring out knights and bishops early instead of moving the same piece twice.
  • Castle early. Castling keeps your king safe and brings a rook into play.
  • Check every capture. Before you move, ask: what can my opponent take, and what can I take?
  • Watch your clock. In fast games, a good move played in time beats a perfect move played too late.
