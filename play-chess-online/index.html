Frequently asked questions

Is it free to join ChessWin7?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and you can practise against the AI at any time.

Do I need to download an app?

No. ChessWin7 runs in your web browser on Android, iPhone, tablets and computers. Just open chesswin7.com.

Can I play chess with a friend?

Yes. Choose vs Friend, create a room and send your friend the 6-character room code. They enter the code and the game starts.

How do you stop cheating?

Every move is checked by our game server and the clocks are kept by the server, so a browser cannot change a move or a result. Engines, bots, multiple accounts and collusion are against our Fair Play Policy.

How do withdrawals work?

The minimum withdrawal is 10 USDT and your email must be verified. A 5% platform fee applies, and requests are reviewed and paid within 48 hours. See the Withdrawal Policy.
