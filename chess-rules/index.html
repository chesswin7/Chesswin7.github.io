Chess Rules for Beginners

Chess is played by two players on an 8×8 board. White moves first, then the players take turns. The goal is to checkmate the opponent's king.

How the pieces move

The dots in each picture show every square the piece can move to.

Check and checkmate

A king that is attacked is in check and must get out of check on the next move: move the king, block the attack, or capture the attacking piece.

If there is no way to escape, it is checkmate and the game is over. In the picture, the white rook gives a “back-rank mate”: the black king is trapped behind its own pawns.

Special moves

How a game can be drawn

  • Stalemate — the player to move has no legal move but is not in check (see the picture: Black to move).
  • Agreement — both players agree to a draw.
  • Threefold repetition — the same position appears three times.
  • 50-move rule — 50 moves by each side without a capture or a pawn move.
  • Insufficient material — neither side has enough pieces left to checkmate.

How much is each piece worth?

A common guide for beginners when deciding whether a trade is good:

PieceValue
♛ Queen9 points
♜ Rook5 points
♝ Bishop3 points
♞ Knight3 points
♟ Pawn1 point
♚ KingPriceless — losing it loses the game
