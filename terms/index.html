Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

ChessWin7 (chesswin7.com) is operated by Zed-Alpha. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

1. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and allowed to take part in skill-based games with real-value deposits under the laws of the place where you live. You are responsible for checking this. One account per person.

2. Skill-based play

ChessWin7 is a skill-based chess platform. Game results depend only on the moves the players make. Every move is checked by our game server under the official rules of chess.

3. Coins, deposits and withdrawals

1 COIN = 1 USDT. Deposits are credited after we have verified the transaction on the blockchain. Withdrawals are described in the Withdrawal Policy. We will never ask you to pay any extra fee, tax or deposit to unlock a withdrawal.

4. Game entries

Every online game against another player (vs Friend or Matchmaking) has an entry fee that both players pay. The entry is taken from your available balance when the game starts and is shown as locked (EXP) while the game is played.

  • Win: you receive both entries minus the platform fee (with the current 5% fee, a 5-COIN entry pays the winner 9.50 COINS).
  • Loss: your entry goes to the winner.
  • Draw: both entries are returned in full.
  • Aborted game (it ends before both players have made a move): both entries are returned in full.
  • If a game is interrupted by a problem on our side, both entries are returned, unless the result was already decided, in which case it is paid out.

Money waiting for a pending withdrawal cannot be used as an entry. Games against the AI are free practice and pay no prizes.

5. Fees

A platform fee (currently 5%) is taken from the prize pool of every won game and from withdrawals. The amounts are shown before you confirm. Draws and aborted games have no fee.

6. Fair play

Engines, bots, outside help, multiple accounts, collusion and any attempt to manipulate results are forbidden. See the Fair Play Policy.

7. Suspension

We may suspend or close accounts involved in fraud, cheating or abuse. Balances linked to fraud may be withheld while we investigate.

8. Changes and contact

We may update these terms; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: Support.chesswin7@gmail.com
