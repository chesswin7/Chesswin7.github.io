How We Keep Every Game Fair

By the ChessWin7 Team · 4 November 2026

When there is something at stake, fairness matters more than anything. This is how ChessWin7 protects every game.

Every move is checked by our server

Your browser only shows the board. Each move is sent to our game server, which checks it against the official rules of chess before your opponent sees it. Illegal moves are impossible, and a modified browser can’t change the position.

The clock belongs to the server

The server keeps the official clock for both players. Refreshing the page, a slow phone or a changed computer clock can’t give anyone extra time. If your opponent runs out of time but you don’t have enough pieces left to checkmate, the game is a draw — exactly as in the official rules.

Only the server writes results and payouts

Wins, losses, ratings and prize payouts are written by the server, never by a browser. Entries are locked and paid out with secure database transactions, so every game is settled exactly once.

Fair play rules

  • No chess engines, bots or outside help during a game.
  • One account per person — no playing against yourself.
  • No agreeing results with another player.

Accounts that break these rules may be suspended. Read the full Fair Play Policy.

Your account is protected

Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase, and the whole site uses an encrypted HTTPS connection. We will never ask for your password, and never ask you to pay a fee to unlock a withdrawal.

Seen something wrong?

Email Support.chesswin7@gmail.com with the player’s name and the time of the game. Every report is looked into.
