How to Win More Blitz Chess Games

By the ChessWin7 Team · 4 November 2026

In blitz (3 or 5 minutes per player) the best move is the good move you play in time. These eight tips are about turning your chess into results under the clock.

1. Play openings you know

Have one opening for White and one answer to 1.e4 and 1.d4, and play the first moves quickly. Every second you save in the opening is a second you can spend in the middlegame. Our guide to the best chess openings for beginners is a good place to start.

2. Budget your time

In a 3-minute game you have about 4–5 seconds per move for a 40-move game. Think longer only at the key moments — when the position changes, or before a big exchange — and move quickly when the move is obvious.

3. Keep your pieces protected

Unprotected pieces are the first to drop in fast games. Before you move, check that every piece is defended, or at least not attacked.

4. Simple moves are good moves

When you are unsure, choose the safe, natural move: develop a piece, castle, improve your worst piece. Long calculations cost time and often aren't needed.

5. Play to the clock situation

  • You have more time: keep the position complicated and make your opponent think.
  • You are ahead in material: trade pieces and simplify into an easy ending.
  • You are short of time: keep your king safe, avoid tactics and play fast, safe moves.

6. Watch your back rank

In fast games, back-rank mates happen all the time. Give your king a little space — a pawn move like h3 (or h6 for Black) — once the opening is over.

7. Know the basic endgames

Many blitz games are decided in the last minute. Practise mating with king and queen, and with king and rook, until you can do it in a few seconds.

8. Take breaks

After two or three losses in a row, stop for a while. Tilt makes you play faster and worse. Playing rested is the easiest rating boost there is — and part of playing responsibly.
