5 Best Chess Openings for Beginners

By the ChessWin7 Team · 4 November 2026

You don't need dozens of openings. Learn one for White, one answer to 1.e4 and one answer to 1.d4 — and learn the ideas, not long lists of moves.

1. Italian Game (for White)

One of the oldest openings and perfect for learning. White develops quickly, gets ready to castle and puts pressure on f7. Typical plans: castle, play c3 and d4 to build a strong centre.

2. Queen’s Gambit (for White)

It isn't a real gambit: if Black takes on c4, White usually wins the pawn back while building a big centre with e4. The Queen’s Gambit teaches you how pawns fight for the centre.

3. London System (for White)

Popular because you can play the same setup — d4, Bf4, e3, Nf3, Bd3, Nbd2, c3 — against nearly every reply. That means less to memorise and more time to understand the middlegame.

4. Sicilian Defence (for Black against 1.e4)

The most popular answer to 1.e4 at every level. The position becomes unbalanced, which gives Black real winning chances. It is sharp, so learn the main ideas before playing it in fast games.

5. Caro-Kann Defence (for Black against 1.e4)

A great choice if you prefer solid positions. Black’s structure is hard to break, and the light-squared bishop usually gets out before Black plays e6.

How to choose

  • Like quick development and attacks? Try the Italian Game.
  • Prefer a fixed plan? The London System.
  • As Black, want to win? The Sicilian. Want to be solid? The Caro-Kann.
